G.H.O.S.T.

A modular agentic AI platform for national security and enterprise missions.

G.H.O.S.T. (Guided-Heuristic On-Prem Support & Troubleshooting) hosts a library of mission-specific AI agents that run multi-step workflows without data leaving your environment.

  • Modular AI agents for domain-specific, one-click automated workflows, with no prompt engineering
  • Runs on-premises, in a private cloud, hybrid or fully disconnected, with an optional zero-connectivity, no-call-home design
  • On-device fine-tuned language models, or models in your own private cloud tenant
  • Reads PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text and images, and cites the source document for every output
  • Containerization and encryption meeting DoD and IC requirements, with role-based access, single sign-on and audit logging
  • More than 30 mission agents, including BATTLEMIND for mission planning, NODE for network operations, TALON for cyber threat intelligence and ARIA for post-award acquisition
  • AI Council: specialist agents deliberate on one tasking and return one advisory verdict, with dissent recorded

Platform capabilities

Seven capabilities sit on top of the agents.

They are how G.H.O.S.T. plans, checks its own work, remembers, and decides where each task should run.

01

AI Council

Convene more than one expert.

Specialist agents, each able to run on its own model, deliberate in front of you and return one verdict. Advisory only, and dissent stays visible.

02

M.A.C.S.

Wargame the plan before the world does.

A friendly agent built from your doctrine and orders fights an adversary armed with what you hold on it, round after round, and leaves a written record. The same engine can wargame your company against its competitors.

03

Agentic Loop

One prompt, many rounds.

The agent plans its own lines of inquiry, searches and reads your documents and trusted sources, checks what it still lacks, then assembles the product with sources cited and gaps stated, not filled in.

04

Smart Router

The right model, automatically.

Every request is scored for reasoning depth and sent to a small model on your server, a large model on your server, or an off-site model, under your policy and spend limit. Sensitive values are tokenized before anything leaves.

05

Memory

Every session starts where the last one ended.

Captures corrections, decisions and conventions. Personal memory is yours to edit, nothing enters team memory until a curator promotes it, and doctrine and policy always outrank memory.

06

Hunter

It does not wait for an alert.

Maps your logs, SIEM export, database or API, sweeps every record with detectors that cost nothing to run, shows a model only the candidates that survive, and runs down each confirmation in its own sub-hunt.

07

G.H.O.S.T. Mode

One request. The right agents.

Detects the mission domain, assembles the specialist agents, binds each to the workflow that fits its part, and returns one cited deliverable with assumptions and gaps listed. It asks rather than assumes.

C.O.S.M.O

Cognitive Operations & Security Monitoring Orchestrator. The AI-driven cyber intelligence platform: its AI does the heavy lifting of a security team, from the first alert to the fix, and your analysts approve.

  • Triages the whole alert queue for you: decides what is real, what can be closed and what needs a closer check
  • Assigns the work automatically: the right team and the right person, each with a ready-made work pack
  • Builds rapid-response playbooks and the fixes teams paste straight in: firewall blocks, detection rules, Splunk and Defender searches
  • Takes malware apart with a council of five AI analysts and writes the rules that catch it
  • Shows the reasons behind every score, and nothing changes until a person approves
  • Runs on your own servers, fully offline if needed

Platform capabilities

Seven capabilities behind every incident.

They are how C.O.S.M.O takes an alert from first sighting to an approved fix.

01

C.O.S.M.O Bot

It does the work. You approve it.

Triages the whole alert queue, scores each incident with every reason shown, assigns it to the right team and person, and builds a work pack for each team. Nothing changes until a person presses Approve.

02

Bring your own data

Reads what your tools already produce.

Firewall and IDS logs, Zeek, CEF and LEEF, syslog, documents, mailboxes and web pages. Splunk, Elastic and Sentinel are searched in place, so nothing is copied.

03

Hunt

Find what no alert caught.

Suggests where to look for the groups and malware already in your queue, then sweeps your logs and computers for traces. A hunt only reads, and anything it finds opens as a case.

04

Respond

A response plan, waiting for approval.

Lays out the steps to stop an attack and who does each one: firewall rules for six makes, isolating a machine, collecting evidence. Nothing runs until a person approves it.

05

Scope & Impact

See how close an attacker is.

Maps every machine by how many steps it sits from your most valuable systems and draws the paths an attacker could take to reach them.

06

Malware Lab

Five AI analysts take malware apart.

A council of five AI analysts, each with one job, explains what a file does and writes the rules that catch it. Files are read, never run.

07

Proof of the work

Every answer comes with its working.

Every score shows what went into it. Each team gets one PDF work order, with firewall commands and detection rules in Suricata, Sigma, YARA, Splunk and Microsoft KQL.
