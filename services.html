Services

Comprehensive technology solutions.

From aviation safety to military intelligence, we deliver mission-critical solutions for the Department of Defense and Intelligence Community.

Aviation Safety

Cypher is the prime contractor on aviation and transportation safety programs for the Federal Aviation Administration, the U.S. Navy and the Department of Transportation. Our teams manage the programs, engineer and build the software behind safety data, analyze that data, run operations and maintenance, and train the people who use it.

  • Program management
  • Engineering
  • Analytics
  • Software development
  • Operations and maintenance
  • Training

FAA Aviation Safety Action Program: safety reporting platform

Cypher designs, builds, hosts and supports a web-based safety reporting platform used across the aviation industry. It is offered to air carriers, repair stations, manufacturers and other operators at no licensing cost. More than 200 operators and over 350,000 users use it to report safety concerns, assess risk, run audits and investigations, and track corrective actions. We keep it running around the clock on secure cloud hosting, extend it with mobile reporting, analytics and dashboards, train its users and host industry forums.

FAA Office of NextGen: management technology support

Cypher runs the business systems behind the office that manages NextGen's budget, contracts, grants and staffing. We modernize and maintain its applications, including moving legacy tools onto Microsoft Power Platform, and turn agency financial and procurement data into reports and dashboards for leaders. The work includes helpdesk support, user access management, plain-language technical writing and security accreditation under FAA and NIST standards.

U.S. Navy: naval aviation program support

Cypher supports a Navy aviation program office with planning, risk management and configuration management for aerial refueling and fuel tank systems. We prepare leadership briefings and planning documents, track engineering changes and technical directives, coordinate multi-site teams and fleet working groups, and keep the program's safety incident data up to date.

Federal Transit Administration: Safety Management Systems outreach

Cypher helps the FTA's Office of System Safety put Safety Management Systems into practice at transit agencies nationwide. We produce workshops, webinars, guides, checklists, job aids and multimedia that help agencies meet federal safety requirements. All materials are written in plain language, meet Section 508 accessibility standards and are available in Spanish.

Military Intelligence & Cyber Operations

Cypher puts experienced, cleared intelligence and cyber professionals on mission for the Department of Defense, the Intelligence Community and federal law enforcement. Our analysts, planners and engineers track threats to Defense networks, turn them into actionable intelligence and brief it to decision makers at the highest levels.

  • Cyber intelligence analysis and production
  • Cyber planning and targeting
  • Threat hunting and intrusion analysis
  • Cyber watch operations and network defense
  • HUMINT and SIGINT support
  • Malware analysis and incident response

U.S. Cyber Command

Cypher supports USCYBERCOM's intelligence and operations directorates. Our analysts track threats to the Department of Defense Information Network, spot significant trends in computer network operations and propose new analytic projects to keep the command ahead of emerging developments. Cypher key personnel serve as the intelligence liaison on the Joint Operations Center floor.

U.S. Central Command

For CENTCOM's Joint Intelligence Operations Center, Cypher researches and analyzes transnational cyber threats. We review, edit and publish the network intelligence alerts and bulletins that keep commanders and their staffs informed.

Intelligence for senior leaders

Cypher briefers deliver intelligence on foreign cyber capabilities, vulnerabilities and threat actors to flag officers and senior defense leadership. As daily briefers to senior staff, our people give leaders actionable intelligence to stop malicious activity and network intrusions.

Federal law enforcement

Cypher supports the FBI's cyber mission, sharing significant network threat trends with law enforcement, IT security and Intelligence Community partners so they can act together on network defense.

Cyber Security

Cypher security engineers protect classified and mission-critical networks for the Intelligence Community, U.S. Cyber Command and federal civilian agencies. We build security into every architecture change, assess and authorize systems under the Risk Management Framework, and keep firewalls, servers and cloud environments hardened and compliant.

  • Risk Management Framework (RMF) assessment and authorization
  • System certification and accreditation
  • Risk and vulnerability assessments
  • Cloud and network security controls
  • Firewall and cross-domain solution engineering
  • Security documentation and compliance reporting

Intelligence Community security engineering

Cypher engineers are the technical security advocate for every system architecture change at an Intelligence Community customer. We evaluate classified networks and applications, support cross-domain solutions that link multiple critical networks, and help run security pilots and research and testing.

U.S. Cyber Command

Cypher keeps mission systems accredited and defended. We write and maintain RMF documentation and compliance reports, manage firewall rule sets and access control lists on Cisco and Juniper equipment, and resolve server connectivity across Windows, Unix and Linux.

Federal civilian agencies

For the FAA, Cypher maintains security authorization packages under FAA and NIST standards, assesses the impact of new security policies, and works with agency security offices to remediate findings. Our engineers also harden the Linux servers behind public-facing safety applications.

Built on a secure foundation

Cypher is CMMC ready, NIST SP 800-171 and DFARS compliant, and ISO 9001:2015 certified. Our engineers hold CISSP, CISM, CASP+, CEH, GPEN, GREM and GSEC certifications.

Enterprise Program Management

Cypher runs programs and modernizes how agencies work for the Navy, the FAA, the Federal Transit Administration, the Air Force, the Intelligence Community and NIST. Our program managers and consultants bring structure, clear reporting and steady execution, so leaders always know where their programs stand and what comes next.

  • Program and project management
  • Agile coaching and Scrum consulting
  • Business process improvement
  • Configuration and change management
  • Risk assessment and mitigation
  • Performance metrics and executive reporting

U.S. Navy: naval aviation program management

Cypher supports a NAVAIR program office with day-to-day program management. We manage the program's workflow, build tools that track and predict budget execution, analyze trends in requests from the fleet, and prepare the briefings and planning documents leadership relies on. We also support configuration control, engineering change proposals, risk management and strategic planning.

FAA: program management on prime task orders

On every FAA task order we lead, Cypher manages to PMBOK and Agile best practices. Leaders get weekly and monthly status reports, monthly financial reports, quarterly program reviews and a quality control plan that measures our own performance. Our transition plans bring new work on board without interrupting service.

Federal Transit Administration

Cypher plans and tracks the FTA's safety outreach work with project plans, schedules and budgets for each effort. Monthly progress reports give the agency a clear view of work completed, upcoming activities, risks and recommendations.

Enterprise IT management

Across the Navy, FAA, Air Force, Intelligence Community and NIST, Cypher personnel provide enterprise configuration management, IT strategic planning, requirements management and Agile coaching. We map current and future-state processes, define performance metrics for leadership reporting, and recommend changes that help teams deliver more with the resources they have.

Software Development & DevOps

Cypher builds, modernizes and runs software that federal missions depend on, from industry-wide safety platforms to cloud data pipelines and public websites. We work in Agile teams, deliver to the cloud, and keep security and testing built in from the first requirement to long-term maintenance.

  • Agile application design and development
  • Cloud migration and engineering on AWS and GovCloud
  • DevSecOps and secure Linux hardening
  • Integration of commercial, open-source and custom software
  • Data analytics, machine learning and data modeling
  • Independent testing and lifecycle maintenance

FAA: aviation safety reporting platform

Cypher designs, develops, tests and deploys an open-source, web-based safety platform used by more than 200 aviation operators. We run it around the clock on AWS, add new modules and mobile reporting, connect it to national safety data repositories, and manage every change through a formal review process.

FAA: Spinner 6.0

Cypher built a new reactive, data-driven application framework for Spinner 6.0, shaped by current user feedback. It supports multiple forms of authentication and native mobile apps.

NIST: Federal Laboratory Consortium website

Cypher defined requirements for, designed, developed, tested and deployed the enterprise website for the Federal Laboratory Consortium at federallabs.org. We built it with Java and AngularJS and integrated it with Drupal.

U.S. Air Force: flight data processing in the cloud

Cypher moved a flight data processing engine onto AWS Simple Workflow Service so it could run many more processing servers at once, scaling automatically with load. We added a PostgreSQL catalog of the data stored in S3, documented the new design, and helped move the system to AWS GovCloud.

FAA Office of NextGen: Power Platform modernization

Cypher modernizes the office's business applications, moving legacy SharePoint forms and workflows onto Microsoft Power Platform. We enhance and maintain its tools using SharePoint, Oracle, SQL, .NET and Java.

C.O.S.M.O: Cypher's cyber intelligence platform

Cypher designed and built C.O.S.M.O, an AI-driven cyber threat intelligence and planning platform. It brings internal and commercial threat feeds together, enriches them and automates responses, giving security teams real-time, actionable intelligence. See C.O.S.M.O.
