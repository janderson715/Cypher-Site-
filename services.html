Services

Comprehensive technology solutions.

From aviation safety to military intelligence, we deliver mission-critical solutions for the Department of Defense and Intelligence Community.

Aviation Safety

Cypher supports aviation safety programs for the Federal Aviation Administration and the Air Force (IRIS MFOQA). Our teams manage the programs, engineer and build the software behind aviation safety data, analyze that data, run operations and maintenance, and train the people who use it.

  • Program management
  • Engineering
  • Analytics
  • Software development
  • Operations and maintenance
  • Training

Military Intelligence & Cyber Operations

Cypher delivers comprehensive military intelligence and cyber operations support to the Department of Defense and Intelligence Community. Our team of professionals provides all-source intelligence analysis, cyber threat operations, and mission-critical engineering services that protect national security interests.

  • All-source intelligence collection and analysis
  • Cyber threat operations and engineering
  • Signals intelligence (SIGINT) support
  • Intelligence fusion and dissemination
  • Mission planning and operational support

Cyber Security

Cypher provides end-to-end cybersecurity services that safeguard mission-critical systems and sensitive data. From vulnerability assessments and penetration testing to continuous monitoring and incident response, our team ensures your organization maintains a robust security posture against evolving threats.

  • Vulnerability assessment and penetration testing
  • Security Operations Center (SOC) management
  • Incident response and digital forensics
  • Risk Management Framework (RMF) implementation
  • Continuous monitoring and threat detection

Enterprise Program Management

Cypher brings decades of experience managing large-scale, enterprise-level programs across the Department of Defense and Intelligence Community. Our program managers deliver strategic oversight, risk mitigation, and on-time execution of complex government initiatives while maintaining full compliance with federal acquisition regulations.

  • Program and portfolio management for DoD and IC
  • Acquisition strategy and contract management
  • Risk identification, assessment, and mitigation
  • Earned Value Management (EVM) and performance reporting
  • Agile and hybrid program execution

Software Development & DevOps

Cypher designs and develops secure, scalable software solutions using modern engineering practices. Our DevOps teams implement CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, and cloud-native architectures that accelerate delivery while maintaining the stringent security standards required by government and defense clients.

  • Full-stack application development
  • CI/CD pipeline design and implementation
  • Cloud-native and microservices architecture
  • Platform engineering and SaaS development
  • DevSecOps and secure software lifecycle management

Geospatial & Space Mission Systems

Cypher provides specialized geospatial intelligence (GEOINT) and space mission systems engineering services. Our teams support satellite operations, geospatial data analysis, and mission system integration to deliver actionable intelligence and maintain space domain awareness for national security stakeholders.

  • Geospatial intelligence (GEOINT) analysis and production
  • Satellite operations and mission system engineering
  • Space domain awareness and situational assessment
  • Remote sensing data processing and exploitation
  • GIS platform development and integration
