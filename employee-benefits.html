Employee benefits

We invest in our people.

We do not just hire professionals. We seek out the best in the industry, and we offer a competitive, ever-evolving benefits package that reflects the value our team brings.

  • Generous Paid Time Off

    Competitive PTO policy because balance fuels performance. Recharge and return ready to deliver your best work.

  • 11 Paid Federal Holidays

    Enjoy eleven paid federal holidays each year, giving you time to spend with family and celebrate what matters.

  • 401(k) Retirement Plan

    Plan for long-term success with our 401(k) retirement plan, helping you build financial security for the future.

  • Premium Medical & Dental Insurance

    Comprehensive and competitive medical and dental coverage to keep you and your family healthy and protected.

  • Short & Long-Term Disability

    Short- and long-term disability coverage provides peace of mind and financial protection when you need it most.

  • Company-Sponsored Luncheons

    Regular company-sponsored luncheons that bring the team together, because team culture matters at Cypher.

  • Industry-Leading Referral Bonuses

    We trust your eye for talent. Earn industry-leading referral bonuses when you help us build the best team.

  • Performance-Based Spot Bonuses

    Great work gets recognized. Performance-based spot bonuses reward exceptional contributions as they happen.

  • Paid Birthday Day Off

    Celebrate your birthday your way with a paid day off. A small gesture that shows we value you as a person.

  • Tuition Reimbursement

    Invest in your future with tuition reimbursement for approved continuing education and professional development.

Full benefit details are provided by HR when you join the Cypher team.
